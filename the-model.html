The New Operating Model

Marketing has been restructured. Most companies haven't caught up.

Budgets are flat, agency spend is being cut, and AI has changed what a small team can deliver. Ballintra is built for this environment: executive-led strategy and hands-on execution, run as Modern Marketing Operations rather than headcount or retainers.

This is how Modern Marketing Operations works. Executive-led strategy paired with AI-enabled execution and global talent, assembled as a single accountable unit. No department overhead. No agency markup. Just senior leadership, AI agents that scale, and vetted resources working in concert.

The leader — a founder or operator who has held the P&L — owns the strategy and the outcomes. The AI handles velocity and precision in execution. Global talent delivers craft at speed. The result: enterprise-grade marketing delivered lean.

02The Squeeze

The old way of scaling marketing has stopped working.

For two decades, marketing scaled by adding headcount or agency retainers. That model is breaking, and 39% of CMOs are already cutting agency relationships. Marketing now has to run lean and tie directly to revenue.

Source: Gartner 2025 CMO Spend Survey.

03The Leverage

AI-enabled workflows change what a small team can deliver.

The answer to a flat budget isn't more hours, it's a different way of working. McKinsey estimates generative AI could unlock $463 billion in annual value across marketing and sales alone. Paired with global talent, lean teams now do full-department work.

Source: McKinsey & Company, The economic potential of generative AI.

04The Shift

Discovery is moving from search to AI answer engines.

Buyers now discover vendors through AI assistants and generative search, not blue links. Competition shifts from raw spend to relevance inside AI-powered ecosystems. Being legible to models is now part of good positioning and PR.

Source: McKinsey & Company, Where AI will create value — and where it won't (2025).
