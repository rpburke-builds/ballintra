About

An operator's approach to digital growth and strategy.

Ballintra bridges global digital marketing leadership, corporate communications, and modern AI operating models.

01Why

Marketing and digital execution are too often disconnected from revenue targets. Traditional agencies focus on billable hours and vanity metrics, while advisory firms deliver decks without staying around to execute.

Ballintra runs on Modern Marketing Operations: senior marketing leadership, AI-enabled workflows, and vetted global technical and creative talent in one model. Strategy and execution stay in the same hands — from setting go-to-market direction to building the website or cutting the video — without the cost or bloat of a large in-house team.

02Principles

Grounded in GrowthEvery campaign, piece of content, and digital strategy links back to revenue or deal metrics.

No JargonClear strategic memos and plain-English roadmaps that sales teams and boards actually use.

Modern Marketing OperationsAI-enabled workflows and global talent scale output dramatically, so a lean team delivers like a full department.

Operator Leadership25+ years on the operator side of the desk building digital functions through rapid growth and downturns.
